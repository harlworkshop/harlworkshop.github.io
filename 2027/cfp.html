Prospective participants in the workshop are required to submit a contribution as:

- Extended abstract (max 2 pages)
- Full paper (max 6 pages)

Submission formatting, reference page limits and review anonymity will be announced with the final call.

Send your PDF manuscript through EasyChair: [link to be activated once proposal is accepted].

Contributions are non-archival. Work must not have been previously published in substantially the same form. Three best paper nominees will give six-minute contributed talks. Other accepted works will be presented as posters with one-minute flash talks. Accepted contributions will be hosted on the workshop website.

Each submission will be reviewed by three program committee members for relevance, technical soundness, scientific contribution and coverage of prior work.

Important dates:

Paper and extended abstract submission deadline: Late February 2027 (tentative; exact date to be announced).
Notification of acceptance: Early April 2027 (tentative; exact date to be announced).
Camera-ready version: To be announced.
Workshop: ICRA 2027; date and venue to be announced. Half day, preferably Monday afternoon.
